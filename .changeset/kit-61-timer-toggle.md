`CollapseIcon` joins `ExpandIcon`: both draw in the text color, are hidden from screen readers (the control that draws them carries the name), and take an optional `size`. The modern timer's toggle is named "Show timer details" or "Hide timer details", tells whether it is unfolded, and folds back with the collapse icon instead of a hyphen.
