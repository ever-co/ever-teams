The basic, custom, essential and extra timers draw their own surface: a card with a ring and a soft shadow in the kit's tokens, so the widget reads the same in light and dark on any host page. The digits use the text color with tabular, lining figures. The basic timer's play control is a filled chip in the brand color that turns red with a stop sign while the timer runs (the other three keep their own icons). `background="none"` (with `rounded="none"`) gives back the bare digits of earlier versions.
