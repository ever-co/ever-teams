`<ever-teams-timer sign-in="ever-teams" organization="..." app-url="...">` signs people in with Ever Teams in a popup, with no password or token typed in the page. Without `sign-in`, or with another value, the element keeps the kit's own sign-in. The shipped declaration types the three attributes, also in camelCase (`signIn`, `organization`, `appUrl`).
